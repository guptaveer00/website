Read the methodology & limitations

Monthly adjusted prices from Yahoo Finance approximate reinvested distributions and split adjustments. Holdings are model units, not actual historical share counts. Contributions enter at each subsequent month observation. Scheduled rebalancing starts 3 or 12 months after your chosen start; drift rules use that month's observed prices. Trades incur proportional costs on both buys and sells. Cash earns 0%; taxes, inflation, bid/ask spreads, and intramonth changes are excluded.

Annual return is time-weighted: monthly portfolio returns remove external contributions before compounding. Volatility is the population standard deviation of monthly returns × √12. Drawdown uses the compounded return index so deposits do not hide losses. The SPY benchmark uses identical deposits and fees. Current partial months are excluded. The ETF selection introduces survivorship bias; testing the same history repeatedly can overfit. Historical performance does not predict future performance.

*The available history depends on the cached data snapshot. Educational simulation only.

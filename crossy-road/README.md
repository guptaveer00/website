# Crossy Campus
Get the character to class on time; avoid and dodge all the obstacles!

## How to play
- Arrow keys or WASD hop one grid square. Hold a key to keep hopping; a tap during a hop queues the next move. 
- Avoid cars, ride moving logs across rivers, and go around trees and rocks. Landing in water or drifting off the playable river edge ends the run.
- Each completed forward hop earns one point. Backward and sideways hops do not score. The number appears at the top right of the game.
- Touching a car ends the run. Press R or click Restart to play again.
- The camera follows forward progress; you cannot move below the visible board.

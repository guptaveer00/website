## How to play
- Arrow keys or WASD hop one grid square. Hold a key to keep hopping; a tap during a hop queues the next move. On-screen arrow buttons also work.
- Avoid cars, ride moving logs across rivers, and go around trees and rocks. Landing in water or drifting off the playable river edge ends the run.
- Each completed forward hop earns one point, including revisited rows. Backward and sideways hops do not score. The number appears at the top right of the game.
- Touching a car ends the run. Press R or click Restart to play again.
- The camera follows forward progress; you cannot move below the visible board.

## Run
Open index.html directly in a browser. No dependencies, server, or build step are required. All file links are relative for GitHub Pages.

Angled 2.5D graphics, animated hops, and smooth camera tracking are implemented. Roads, rivers with moving logs, and blocking trees and rocks are implemented. Sound and saved high scores are not implemented. Crosswalks are decorative: cars do not stop for them. Live GitHub Pages testing is still pending.

About

Statistics, probability, and building things that trade.

A bit more on my background, what I'm focused on, and what I do outside of markets.

I'm a sophomore at Carnegie Mellon University pursuing a BS in Statistics and Data Science with a Minor in Computational Finance. I'm on track to graduate in 2029, with early graduation in 2028 possible depending on how the next couple of years go.

Most of my time outside class goes into building and testing trading systems — from a live algorithmic operation on Kalshi's prediction markets to derivatives research on volatility signals around FOMC events. I like problems where you have to think in probabilities and sizes, not certainties, which is a big part of why I'm drawn to quant trading, market making, and research roles.

I'm also Vice President of Media and Outreach and a trader with Traders at CMU, where I help run a 200+ member trading club and build systematic factor models with the trading team.
